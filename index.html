A note from the founder

Sarah M. Gordon
Principal · FiscalAI

I spent a decade inside the boardrooms of global titans Mastercard and Ameriprise Financial, translating complex financial data into the executive narratives that drive corporate performance.

Independent founders deserve that same visibility. Without it, the numbers can look strong on the surface while margins, cash timing, and risk stay out of view. I call it surface-level solvency.

That is why I built FiscalAI as a complete suite. LIA keeps your books audit-ready every day of the year. Steward reads them the way a CFO would and tells you what comes next. The downloads put proven financial frameworks in your hands the same afternoon, and when your business calls for a seasoned analyst, you can work with me directly. Wherever your business stands today, there is a place to begin and a clear path to financial confidence.

At the center of it all is Steward. He gives founders the full depth of a finance department before they're ready to hire one: analyzing historical performance, reading current cash position, and modeling forecasts across best- and worst-case scenarios. The result is margin clarity, early sight of risk, and numbers you can count on to make confident decisions.

My commitment is simple: bring the clarity, stability, and control of Fortune 100 finance to the global titans of tomorrow.
