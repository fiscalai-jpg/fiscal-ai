Education & Development

Finance Mentorship Program

A structured 6-module curriculum for college finance students — taught through systems logic, not memorization. Real models. Real deliverables. A real credential.

01
Financial Statement Architecture
Three-statement systems logic — why cash and accruals diverge before a single formula is touched.
02
Three-Statement Integration Model
Build a fully linked IS/BS/CF from a blank Excel sheet. No templates. Balance sheet must balance.
03
DCF Valuation
WACC construction, terminal value, and a sensitivity table. Students defend every assumption verbally.
04
Comparable Company Analysis
Peer set selection, EV/EBITDA multiples, and a one-page valuation bridge with written commentary.
05
Advanced Excel & Data Tools
XLOOKUP, VBA macros, Power Query, pivot tables. Students present a live dashboard without notes.
06
LBO Fundamentals
Conceptual literacy on leveraged buyout structure, IRR drivers, and debt capacity logic.
