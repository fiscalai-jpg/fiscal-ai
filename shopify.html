How this is calculated
  • Monthly profit = sales × what you keep − monthly business costs, then your tax set-aside comes off the top.
  • The safe paycheck is the smaller of two limits: 85% of an average month's profit (15% stays in the business to grow your reserve), and what your slowest month earns plus enough of today's cash to cover three slow months.
  • One month of business costs always stays in the account and is never counted toward your pay.
  • Figures round down to the nearest $50.
